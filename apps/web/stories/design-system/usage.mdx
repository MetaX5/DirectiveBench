# Usage and accessibility

The rules every screen follows, each with its source.

## Keyboard and focus

1. Everything that acts is reachable with Tab and works with Enter (and Space for buttons).
   Use the components: native elements bring this for free. Source: WCAG 2.1.1, SPEC-121.
2. Focus is always visible: `border-ring` plus a solid 1px ring. Source: tokeny.md D4, WCAG
   2.4.7.
3. Focus order follows reading order; no positive `tabIndex`. Scrolling blocks (`Pre`, wide
   tables) are tab stops. Source: WCAG 2.4.3, SPEC-122 P13.
4. After a submit with errors, focus moves to the first invalid field. Source: Form pattern.
   The artifact editing forms do this; `/repositories` does not yet (What is missing).
5. When the focused control disappears (its item was removed or unassigned), focus moves to
   the field that adds items to that list, never to the page body. After an item is added,
   focus stays in that field, ready for the next one. When that field is gone or disabled
   (the last free suite was assigned, the list is full), focus moves to the item just added.
   A button disabled while it saves loses focus: return it to the form's field. Source: WCAG 2.4.3, issue 64
   (`app/artifacts/[id]/metadata-forms.tsx`).

## Forms and messages

5. Every field has a visible label; the placeholder is an example. Source: WCAG 3.3.2.
6. Field errors sit under the field with `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby` and
   `role="alert"`; general errors go above the form. Source: WCAG 3.3.1, Form pattern.
7. Status and progress use `role="status"`; they never take focus. Keep the `role="status"`
   element rendered and change its text: a live region inserted together with its message is
   often not announced. Source: WCAG 4.1.3, WAI-ARIA live regions.
8. Loading: disable the control and say what is happening; no spinner. Source: SPEC-122 P11.
9. A disabled control says why next to it, linked with `aria-describedby`. Source: WCAG 3.3.2,
   Button page.

## Colour and contrast

10. Text 4.5:1, large text and UI parts 3:1, in both themes, on the real surface. Source:
    AGENTS.md §7, Colour page.
11. Colour never carries meaning alone: a word or a sign goes with it. Source: WCAG 1.4.1.
12. Status colours are fills; their text is `*-foreground`. Source: tokeny.md D5.

## Motion

13. Colour and opacity transitions only, 150ms at most, none under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
    Source: SPEC-122 Q2, P9.
14. Pages that follow a job re-render every 2 seconds through `AutoRefresh`
    (`components/auto-refresh.tsx`), which renders nothing and stops when no job is active.
    Nothing moves; only the text changes. It has no stories because it has no output.

## Content

15. App-authored text has no en or em dash. Imported content and raw evidence stay verbatim.
    Source: AGENTS.md §7, issue 123.
16. Tone and wording: see Content.

## Widths

17. Every screen works at 320px, 768px and 1440px; at 390px badges wrap and nothing is clipped.
    Tables scroll inside their container, never the page. Source: SPEC-119 FR-7, Spacing and
    layout.
18. Interactive targets are at least 24 by 24px. Source: WCAG 2.5.8.

## Product rules

19. Every mock-agent result carries `MockBadge`. Source: AGENTS.md §7, SPEC-101 FR-101-14.
20. Planned features say "Planned: not implemented", with no buttons that do nothing. Source:
    AGENTS.md §7, SPEC-001 principle 3.
21. The web app reads and enqueues; it never runs processes or benchmarks. Source: AGENTS.md
    §5, SPEC-003.
22. Untrusted content (artifacts, agent output) is shown as text: `Markdown` and `Pre`, never
    `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`. Source: SPEC-101 FR-101-18, AGENTS.md §7.
