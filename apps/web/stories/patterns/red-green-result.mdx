# RED/GREEN result

Modelled on `/results/[id]` (SPEC-112, SPEC-118). The same suite runs without the artifact
(baseline, RED) and with it (candidate, GREEN); the verdict compares them. Benchmarking first
is product decision D08.

## Structure

`PageHeader` (suite and run, `StatusBadge`, `MockBadge` or the agent, `NotSandboxedBadge`) > a
note for mock runs > two cards, baseline and candidate, with the score as a metric > the
verdict card with the change and the confidence.

## Rules

1. A mock run carries `MockBadge` in the header and a sentence that the scores show the
   pipeline, not an AI model. Source: AGENTS.md §7, SPEC-113 FR-113-16.
2. The verdict is a word (`StatusBadge`) and a signed number; colour only repeats it. Source:
   WCAG 1.4.1.
3. Unstable and not-comparable results say why and what to do. Source: SPEC-111, SPEC-122 §18
   Q1.
4. Scores use `text-metric tabular-nums` and two decimals.
5. ScoreBar, VariantCard and AssertionDetails are locked in `app/results/[id]/page.tsx`; this
   pattern uses `Card` until they are extracted (on "What is missing").
