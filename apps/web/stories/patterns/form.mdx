# Form

Modelled on "Register a repository" (`/repositories`). The app posts forms to server actions
and re-renders the page; the stories show each submit state through props.

## Structure

`Card` > `CardHeader` (what the form does, in the title and description) > `CardContent` >
general error, the form row (labelled fields, then the one `default` button), the field error,
the status message.

## Rules

1. The label is always visible; the placeholder is an example. Source: WCAG 3.3.2, Input page.
2. A field error sits under its field, is linked with `aria-describedby`, uses
   `aria-invalid` and `role="alert"`. Source: WCAG 3.3.1, `app/repositories/page.tsx`.
3. After a submit with errors, focus moves to the first invalid field. Source: SPEC-122 prompt
   (stage 3); WCAG 3.3.1. The app does not do this yet: on "What is missing".
4. Entered data is kept after an error (`defaultValue` from the server's answer). Source:
   `app/repositories/page.tsx`.
5. While the submit runs, the field and the button are disabled and a `role="status"` line says
   what is happening; a second press does nothing. Source: SPEC-122 §18 P11.
6. An error that is not about one field goes above the form, says what happened and what to do.
   Source: SPEC-122 §18 Q1.
7. Success is a `role="status"` line that names the result and the next step; no toast.
8. One `default` button per form. Source: SPEC-122 §18 P12.
