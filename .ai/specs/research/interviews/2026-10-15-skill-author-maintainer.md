# Interview — skill author and DirectiveBench maintainer, 2026-10-15

Captured during an `om-discover` session from the maintainer's own written answers. The
participant is also the person building DirectiveBench, so this is one account from the builder,
not independent user research.

- Recent situation or task the person described: updating their own agent skills and trying the
  updated skills in a client project.
- What they did, step by step: tested the updated skill "in a real project" (a client project).
  No further steps were described.
- What worked, what did not, and any consequences they described: "its hard to tell if its better
  or worse after the skill update"; "it takes lot of time to figure it out"; "i can see that
  skills dont work as expected after agent fails".
- Other approaches they tried and how those worked: none described. They named Promptfoo and
  SkillBench as similar tools but said they had not checked them.
- The outcome they were trying to achieve and what happened: to know whether a skill update made
  the agent's work better or worse; they found out mainly when the agent failed.
- Verbatim quotes worth keeping (mark each as quote):

    to figure it out."
